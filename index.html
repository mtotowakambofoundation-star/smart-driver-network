TANZANIA'S PROFESSIONAL DRIVER NETWORK

Connecting Professional Drivers with Trusted Employers

Smart Driver Network (SDN) is a digital platform designed to connect qualified professional drivers with employers looking for the right driver for the right vehicle and opportunity.

I'm a Driver I'm an Employer

Why SDN?

  • Professional driver registration
  • Driver document verification
  • Vehicle-specific driver matching
  • Employer driver requests
  • Growing network across Africa

How SDN Works

A simple process for drivers and employers.

01

Driver Registers

A professional driver creates an SDN profile and provides personal, licence, experience and qualification information.

02

Documents Are Verified

SDN reviews submitted driver information and documents before the profile can receive verified status.

03

Employers Find Drivers

Companies and organisations can submit driver requirements and SDN can match them with suitable registered drivers.
